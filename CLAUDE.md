# Working agreements

## Response format

End every response with a `TL;DR` section containing:

1. **TL;DR** — the full picture in 2-4 sentences. Written for a C-suite reader:
   what was done, what the outcome was, and anything that is now blocked,
   at risk, or awaiting a decision. Someone who reads only this section
   should not be missing anything that would change what they do next.
   No jargon without a plain-language gloss.
2. **Next step** — one concrete recommended action, and the reason it is the
   right one. Recommend; do not present a menu of options.

This applies to every response, including short ones and follow-up answers.
